## 🧭 Overview

This e-portfolio showcases my learning and development during the **Work Readiness Training** and **WIL Project** at CPUT.  
It includes evidence of communication, teamwork, leadership, professional behavior, and self-reflection using the **STAR Technique (Situation, Task, Action, Result)**.

As a group leader, I presented most of our project updates, managed meetings, and maintained professional communication across the team.  
Each section below contains artefacts and reflections that demonstrate my personal and professional growth.
